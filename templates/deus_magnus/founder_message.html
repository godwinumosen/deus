Who We Are


More Than Construction. An Integrated Approach to the Built Environment.

At Deus Magnus, we are committed to redefining excellence in Nigeria's construction and real estate industry. Since our inception in 2018, we have rapidly established ourselves as the premium integrated solutions provider, Deus Magnus is an integrated construction, real estate and facility management company delivering solutions across Nigeria's built environment. Our multidisciplinary team brings together professionals across engineering, architecture, estate surveying and valuation, structural design, project management, and related disciplines to transform ideas into functional, high-quality spaces. From development and construction to property management and project support, we approach every engagement with one goal: Delivering service par excellence.

Mr. Kuteyi Rasaq
CEO, Deus Magnus Limited

Vision

To be a leading integrated construction, real estate, and facility management company in Nigeria, delivering innovative, sustainable, and high-quality solutions that enhance the built environment and quality of life. To emerge as the leading construction and real estate service provider in Africa and globally, accomplished through the delivery of internationally acclaimed structures.

Mission

To provide exceptional services, exceeding client expectations, while fostering a culture of safety, integrity, and community engagement. To revolutionize the Nigerian real estate landscape by delivering unparalleled quality through cutting-edge innovation, superior quality, and unwavering commitment to sustainability.

Core Values

  • Integrity:

    • We act with honesty and transparency, earning trust through ethical and responsible conduct.

  • Excellence:

    • Quality is non-negotiable for us. From design to delivery, we focus on every detail and consistently meet required standards.

  • Safety First:

    • People matter most to us. We create safe working environments and strictly follow safety practices to protect our team members, clients and the wider community.

  • Innovation:

    • We constantly explore new ideas and approaches, using modern technologies and creative solutions to achieve better outcomes.

  • Sustainability:

    • We build with the future in mind by using resources responsibly and delivering projects that create long-term value for people and the environment.

  • Professionalism:

    • We uphold high standards of competence, respect, and reliability.

Approach

At Deus Magnus, we address complex challenges facing high-net-worth property developers, investors, and owners. Our innovative approach significantly reduces risks, delays, and premature deterioration by focusing on five key areas:

  • Integrated Solutions Model
  • Lifecycle Cost Optimization
  • Technology Integration
  • Sustainability Focus
  • Quality Assurance and Project Management
